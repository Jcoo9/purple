Privacy
Your privacy matters.
Last updated: June 2026
Purple is a civic polling platform. We are committed to protecting your privacy.
What we collect
When you vote, we record your vote and the time it was cast. If you choose to share demographic information (state, age range, community type, professional background), we record that too. If you sign in with your email, we store that email. Nothing else.
What we never do
We never sell your data. We never show advertising. We never share your email with third parties. We never use your data to target you politically or commercially. No individual vote is ever attributed to you publicly.
How your data is used
Your votes and demographic information are used exclusively to generate aggregated, anonymous statistics showing what Americans from different backgrounds think about different issues.
Data storage
All data is stored securely on Supabase, a US-based cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Votes cast before signing in are stored locally on your device only.
Deleting your data
To delete your account and all data, email privacy@purple-poll.com with subject "Delete my account." We will respond within 7 days.
Contact
Questions? Email privacy@purple-poll.com
Purple exists to help Americans hear each other. Your trust makes that possible. We will never betray it.

How It Works
One vote per person per question
Each device can vote once per topic. Votes are stored in a secure database with timestamps. Nothing is edited after submission.
Results are unfiltered
What people vote is what you see. No weighting, no adjustment, no editorial influence. The numbers are the numbers.
Not a scientific poll
Purple does not use random sampling. Results reflect people who chose to vote — not a representative sample of all Americans. Small sample sizes mean results will shift as more people participate. We show sample sizes on every result so you can judge for yourself.
The data is auditable
Every result can be exported as a CSV from the Data page. Vote totals accumulate over time — you can see exactly how results shifted as more people weighed in. There is no hidden data.
Left/right arguments are AI summaries
The arguments shown on each question are AI-generated summaries of common perspectives — not official party positions or statements from any organization. They're starting points for thinking, not authoritative sources.

What is Purple?
Purple started with a simple question.
What do real Americans actually think — not what the media says, not what the algorithm feeds you, but honest people giving honest answers?
Purple is a civic polling app built on one belief: that most of us are capable of hearing the other side. That disagreement doesn’t have to mean division. That empathy and debate can coexist.
Every question shows you both arguments — left and right — before you vote. Every result shows you where real people actually stand. No spin. No agenda. Just data.
Not left. Not right. Real.
Purple is dedicated to Matthew Roth — a friend who believed that listening to each other was the most radical thing we could do.
