What we are

Purple is America's voice. Not the media's version of it. Not a politician's version of it. The actual thing — what real people think, in their own words, on the issues that shape their lives.

We built Purple because the loudest voices in America aren't the most representative ones. Cable news tells you what to think. Social media rewards outrage. Polls give you numbers without faces. Nobody was building the thing in the middle — a place where a teacher from Ohio, a veteran from Texas, and a nurse from New Jersey all have exactly the same standing.

That place is Purple.

What we believe

Most Americans are not as divided as the loudest voices make us seem. The gap between left and right is real — but so is the vast middle that rarely gets heard. Purple exists for that middle. And for the edges too, as long as they're honest.

We believe in disagreement without contempt. In opinions without algorithms. In the kind of conversation that used to happen at a kitchen table, a diner counter, or a town hall — before everyone started performing for an audience.
