For years, the loudest voices told you what America thinks.
Cable news. Social media. Politicians. Pundits. All of them speaking for you. None of them asking you.
Purple asks.
One question. Real people. No spin. No agenda. No one telling you what to think before you think it.
You vote. You hear the other side in their own words. You see where America actually stands — not the media's version of it. The real one.
Turns out most Americans don't fit neatly into either box.
Purple proves it.
The Name
Red and blue mixed together make purple. It is not a compromise. It is what you get when you stop pretending only one color is real.
The Origin
Purple was conceived by two friends who believed American public discourse had lost its connection to ordinary people. One of them passed away before seeing it built. Purple carries his conviction that real voices matter — and that the truth lives somewhere in the middle.
