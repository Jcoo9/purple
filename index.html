Our Promise
—No advertising. Ever.
—No selling your individual data. Ever.
—No editorial influence on results. What people say is what you see.
—Results show what Purple users say — not a scientific poll of all Americans.
—Voice recordings are public by design. Your words, attributed to you.
—Every voice carries equal weight.
How It Works
One vote per person per question
Each device can vote once per topic. Votes are stored in a secure database with timestamps. Nothing is edited after submission.
Results are unfiltered
What people vote is what you see. No weighting, no adjustment, no editorial influence. The numbers are the numbers.
Not a scientific poll
Purple does not use random sampling. Results reflect people who chose to vote — not a representative sample of all Americans. Small sample sizes mean results will shift as more people participate. We show sample sizes on every result so you can judge for yourself.
The data is auditable
Every result can be exported as a CSV from the Data page. Vote totals accumulate over time — you can see exactly how results shifted as more people weighed in. There is no hidden data.
Left/right arguments are AI summaries
The arguments shown on each question are AI-generated summaries of common perspectives — not official party positions or statements from any organization. They're starting points for thinking, not authoritative sources.
