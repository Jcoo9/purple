What we are

Purple is America's voice. Not the media's version of it. Not a politician's version of it. The actual thing — what real people think, in their own words, on the issues that shape their lives.

We built Purple because the loudest voices in America aren't the most representative ones. That place is Purple.

"Most Americans are not as divided as the loudest voices make us seem."
— Purple, 2025
The name

Red and blue mixed together make purple. It is not a compromise. It is what you get when you stop pretending only one color is real.

The origin

Purple was conceived by two friends who shared a conviction that American public discourse had lost its connection to ordinary people. One of them passed away before seeing it built. Purple carries his belief that real voices matter, that the truth lives somewhere in the middle.

Our promise

— No advertisements. No political agenda.
— No selling your data. Ever.
— No editorial influence on results.
— Every voice carries equal weight.
